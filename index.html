Missing Data:Foundations and Methods

Lu Mao, PhD

University of Wisconsin–Madison

Statistical inference when the data we observe are only part of the story.

  • Assumptions: missingness mechanisms and identifiability.
  • Algorithms: EM, Bayesian computation, and multiple imputation.
  • Inference: connections between likelihood, weighting, and doubly robust estimating equations.
Start with Chapter 1 →Browse the slides ↓

About the course

For graduate students in statistics and biostatistics with a foundation in mathematical statistics. Familiarity with asymptotic theory and statistical programming is helpful.

Two connected perspectives

Begin with missingness and identifiability, develop likelihood-based methods through EM and imputation, then turn to weighting and doubly robust estimating equations.
